## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Genjutsu AI ("Genjutsu AI", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, account features, payment features, and AI-powered video generation and editing services.

Genjutsu AI is the trading and service name used for this website. Privacy-related notices concerning the operator of the service may be sent to [support@genjutsuai.net](mailto:support@genjutsuai.net).

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

1. **Account information** — such as your name, email address, account identifier, and authentication-related information.
2. **User content** — videos, images, reference media, prompts, generation settings, and other material you submit to create AI outputs.
3. **Generation and transaction data** — task identifiers, model selections, resolution, estimated or charged credits, generation status, order references, and related service records.
4. **Usage and device data** — browser type, device information, IP address, timestamps, pages or features used, diagnostics, and security-related logs.
5. **Cookies and similar technologies** — used for authentication, preferences, analytics, security, and service operation.
6. **Payment-related information** — order status, purchase amount, currency, buyer email, payment-provider identifiers, and refund status. Full payment card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored on our servers.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage accounts.
- Provide AI generation and editing features.
- Upload and transmit media needed for generation.
- Estimate, reserve, charge, restore, and display credits.
- Process one-time purchases, payments, and refunds.
- Provide customer support and respond to requests.
- Maintain service reliability, prevent abuse, investigate fraud, and protect security.
- Diagnose errors and improve performance and user experience.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

## AI Providers and Uploaded Media

Genjutsu AI uses third-party AI infrastructure, including Higgsfield, to perform generation tasks.

When you request a generation, media, prompts, reference images, model parameters, and related generation data may be transmitted directly or indirectly to Higgsfield or another disclosed AI provider as necessary to complete the request.

You should not upload content that you do not have the right to process or content containing sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and lawful for your intended use.

## Payments

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers, including **Waffo Pancake**.

Payment processors may receive information required to complete a transaction, including buyer email, order identifiers, purchase amount, currency, payment method information, fraud-prevention signals, and refund information.

Genjutsu AI does not store complete payment card numbers on its servers.

## Service Providers and Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers only where reasonably necessary to operate the service, including:

- AI model and generation providers such as Higgsfield.
- Payment processors such as Waffo Pancake.
- Hosting, storage, database, authentication, email, analytics, monitoring, and security providers.
- Professional advisers or authorities where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the service.

These providers may process data in countries different from your own.

## Analytics and Cookies

We may use **Google Analytics** and **Plausible Analytics** to understand site usage, diagnose issues, and improve the service. When enabled, these providers may receive technical identifiers, usage events, device information, IP-derived information, and cookie-related data according to their own privacy policies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect authentication or service functionality.

## Data Retention

We retain data according to the following operational schedule unless a longer period is required by law, a payment dispute, fraud investigation, or security incident:

- **Account information:** while your account remains active, then up to 90 days after account deletion.
- **Payment and transaction records:** up to 7 years for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and dispute purposes.
- **Customer-support records:** up to 2 years after the support matter is closed.
- **Security and diagnostic logs:** up to 12 months.
- **Generation task metadata:** up to 12 months for history, billing reconciliation, support, and abuse prevention.
- **Uploaded media and generated outputs:** Genjutsu AI does not intentionally keep an additional permanent copy solely for policy purposes. Media sent to third-party AI providers may be retained by those providers under their own operational and privacy policies.

After the applicable period, information is deleted or anonymized where reasonably practicable, unless continued retention is legally required.

## International Data Transfers

Because our service relies on global infrastructure and third-party providers, your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encrypted network transport, access controls, authentication controls, and restricted access to production systems.
