## Prompt and quality tips

**Do**

- Name the subject clearly: “replace the tiptoe dancer with @Image1”
- Keep motion language locked: “same shush, same tiptoe timing, same barn”
- Use one strong photo instead of many weak ones

**Expect some AI limits**

- Lip sync and grin timing are close, not frame-perfect
- Straw texture or costume shine from the template can occasionally bleed through
- Fast toe steps are hard — a sharper source photo helps

If a job fails, retry with a cleaner front-facing photo and a shorter, clearer prompt. Check the on-screen credit estimate before each attempt.
