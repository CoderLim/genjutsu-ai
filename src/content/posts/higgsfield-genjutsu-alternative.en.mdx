## What does Higgsfield Genjutsu do?

Genjutsu is a video-to-video editing workflow. Rather than generating an entirely new clip from a text prompt alone, it starts with **your source footage** and uses that footage as a guide for movement, timing, framing, and camera motion.

Two common editing workflows are:

- **Motion Transfer:** Use the source clip's motion and timing as a reference when changing the visual presentation, scene, or style.
- **Object Swap:** Replace a chosen object or prop in the shot, using a reference image or clear instructions, while aiming to preserve the surrounding scene.

These are not guarantees of pixel-perfect preservation. Fast movement, occlusion, difficult camera angles, or unclear references can change the quality of the result.

### 1. Swap a product in an existing ad

Start with a tracking shot of a bottle or package. Add a reference image of the replacement item and describe the precise change:

> Replace the bottle on the table with the product in the reference image. Keep the original camera move, lighting direction, and shot timing.

The more closely your reference matches the original object's size and camera angle, the easier it is to communicate the intended edit.

### 2. Change the setting while keeping the performance

Use the original clip as a motion guide, then describe a new environment:

> Keep the original movement and camera framing. Restage the scene as a rainy neon-lit street at night.

Treat this as an artistic transformation, not a guarantee that every background detail stays the same.
