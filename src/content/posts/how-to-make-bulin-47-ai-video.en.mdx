## Prompt and quality tips

**Do**

- Name the subject clearly: “replace the freestyle performer with @Image1”
- Keep motion language locked: “same moves, same camera, same timing”
- Use one strong photo instead of many weak ones

**Expect some AI limits**

- Lip sync is close, not frame-perfect
- Crowd faces or jewelry from the template can occasionally bleed through
- Fast hands and mic motion are hard — a sharper source photo helps

If a job fails, retry with a cleaner front-facing photo and a shorter, clearer prompt. Check the on-screen credit estimate before each attempt.
